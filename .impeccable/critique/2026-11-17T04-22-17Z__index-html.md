# Design critique: operations dashboard (`index.html`)

Reviewed the Arabic RTL academy operations prototype before any copy edits. Source checks confirm the count mismatch, the em dashes, and the menu-button CSS. Browser findings come from a separate visual pass on `http://127.0.0.1:8765/index.html` (login, overview, players, player attendance, buses, day close, disconnected players, and a 390px overview).

## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment.** The login would not be called AI-made. The ink panel, the pitch circle, and "كل فرع في الملعب. وكل رقم تحت عينك." are specific. Hidden page eyebrows are set to `display: none`. There is no gradient text, no glass cards as the default surface, and no 01/02/03 scaffolding. Dialogs use a 3px backdrop blur only when they interrupt the sheet.

The overview would be called AI-made. "صباح الخير يا محمد" plus a four-cell scoreboard (label, 32px number, green supporting line) is the hero-metric template: اللاعبون المسجلون 148, اشتراكات نشطة 132, تحصيل الشهر 86%, مقاعد باص متاحة. Under it, an attention strip, a bar chart, a donut, then three panels that repeat big-number-plus-label. Warm paper `#f4f2ea` is the committed canvas in DESIGN.md, so the cream field is an intentional material, and it is still the overview's main tell when paired with those metrics. Role cards, إجراء سريع, and the coach grid repeat icon-plus-heading-plus-text.

**Deterministic scan.** `detect.mjs --json index.html` exited 2 with one finding: `em-dash-overuse` in `index.html` (17 U+2014 characters; the six `--` hits are CSS `--bar` custom properties and are not em dashes). `app.js` returned no CLI findings. This agrees with the copy cadence the review would have under-counted by eye. It is not a false positive: the dashes are in the title, the prototype note, the overview sentence, the chart subtitle, transfer options, the permissions matrix, and the coach-contract options.

**Visual overlays.** No reliable user-visible overlay. Mutation worked, and `http://localhost:8400/detect.js` inserted 16 `.impeccable-overlay` nodes, but 15 were `display: none` and the banner stayed at `translateY(-100%)` (box entirely above the viewport). Screenshots of that overlay timed out. The in-page console hook, which did not include the word "impeccable", reported: `ai-color-palette` 1, `gpt-thin-border-wide-shadow` 1, `hero-eyebrow-chip` 13, `single-font` 1, `cream-palette` 1. The eyebrow and cream hits over-call the committed match-sheet system: eyebrows are hidden, and warm paper is the specified canvas. The single font is required by DESIGN.md. The hero-metric and em-dash findings stand.

## Overall Impression

The login and the day-close ledger are a match sheet. The board cannot trust the sheet, because the same "today" uses three different player counts, and money or a player's membership changes on one click plus a toast.

## What's Working

- Login is a sheet, not a marketing hero: pitch geometry, prefilled demo credentials, and "نموذج واجهة فقط — البيانات المعروضة توضيحية".
- التقفيل اليومي is a real register: time, البيان, method chip, amount, الإداري المستلم, and الفرع. It refuses to close a day with no receipts.
- Branch names are stamped into page lines and closing rows, and bus cards show the 28-seat capacity.

## Priority Issues

- **[P0] The sheet disagrees with itself.** The اللاعبون badge is the literal `248`. The scoreboard and `academy.badr.players` say `148`. The Badr `playersList` has 5 rows. حضور اللاعبين says "4 من 6". A board member cannot close a day on figures that do not match the register. Drive every badge and scoreboard cell from the same list, and replace "البيانات محدثة الآن" with an illustrative-data label. Suggested command: `/impeccable audit` (confirm), then a data-binding fix before `/impeccable polish`.
- **[P0] Money and membership commit in one click.** تقفيل اليوم, إعادة فتح اليوم, إعادة تفعيل, تحضير الكل, and an absence toggle that crosses حد الغياب all finish with a short toast. Name the day and the total before closing, and name the player when an absence reaches the limit. Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`.
- **[P1] The branch control is the first broken control on desktop.** `#menu-btn` has `mobile-only`, and `.mobile-only { display: none }` is set, but the later `.icon-btn { display: inline-flex }` wins on specificity and source order, so the hamburger stays visible and squeezes "الفرع الحالي". The players table then hides الحالة and الملف past the first view. Hide that menu button above 760px with a rule that beats `.icon-btn`, and keep الحالة plus الملف in the first view. Suggested command: `/impeccable layout`.
- **[P2] Two strings fail as type.** The subscription cell can render "شهري" and "متبقي …" as one glued line, and `#annual-budget-year` is `formatNumber(year)`, so 2026 becomes "2,026". Put the remainder on its own line, and do not group the year. Suggested command: `/impeccable typeset`.
- **[P2] False doors.** إضافة مصروف opens المركز المالي, which has no expense form. "صورة حضور تحتاج اعتمادًا" opens حضور اللاعبين, which has no approval control. Photo proof lives on حضور الموظفين. Three money pages share one wallet icon. Cut the false doors. Suggested command: `/impeccable distill`.

## Cognitive load

7 of 8 checks fail: single focus, chunking, grouping, visual hierarchy, one thing at a time, minimal choices, and working memory. Progressive disclosure holds, because nav groups collapse and create/pay flows sit in dialogs. That is high cognitive load.

Decision points with more than 4 visible options:

- Sidebar, 10 top-level buttons.
- Overview, 7 exits (إجراء سريع, three attention rows, فتح سجل الحضور, فتح مرتبات المدربين, عرض التقرير المالي الكامل).
- Desktop top bar plus the page button, 5 controls once the hamburger is visible.

إجراء سريع itself is exactly 4 actions.

## Emotional journey

The peak is the login line, and it happens before any work. تحصيل دفعة is the real reassurance: it states the remainder and ends at حفظ الدفعة وعرض الإيصال. Day close is flatter than the money deserves: 10,000 ج.م can flip from مفتوح to إعادة فتح اليوم on a toast. Disconnecting is a valley: إعادة تفعيل zeroes absences with a toast, and toggling حاضر can push a player onto اللاعبون المنقطعون with no warning. The trust drop is the overview: a green "البيانات محدثة الآن" over figures that do not match.

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (power user).** Escape closes الإشعارات and nothing else. The players filter "كل حالات الاشتراك / نشط / متأخر" has an accessible name and no behavior. تصدير Excel on المركز المالي downloads a CSV. The 248 badge opens a 5-row table. No bulk action on payments or buses.

**Sam (keyboard and screen reader).** Tab order starts in the sidebar, so the nav comes before the workspace. Focus-visible is a 3px orange outline, and dialogs are native `dialog`. Status chips use words as well as color. Red flags: تسجيل الخروج is an arrow with only an accessible name; attendance toggles expose no pressed state; notification time `#9aa59f` on the sheet is about 2.5:1; at 390px the label "الفرع الحالي" is `display: none`, so the control reads as the value "فرع بدر". The status filter is announced and then does nothing.

**Branch administrator (records attendance, coach check-in with photo, buses, and payments).** The work is labeled and then buried. حضور اللاعبين has تحضير الكل. Coach photo evidence is حضور الموظفين. Payments are تحصيل دفعة inside a collapsed group. Buses are ترحيل لاعب بين الباصات. End of day is التقفيل اليومي. Disconnects are اللاعبون المنقطعون. Red flags: the desktop branch label is unreadable while the select still switches the whole book; the attention row about a photo opens the player roster; إضافة مصروف opens a ledger with no form.

## Minor Observations

- "صباح الخير يا محمد" is fixed copy. The sidebar user is "محمد الصعيدي", عضو مجلس إدارة.
- Coach contracts say Full-time and Part-time.
- Finance rows say InstaPay, تحويل بنكي, and بطاقة بنكية. Closing says انستا باي، نقدي، فيزا.
- Age lines repeat themselves: "14 سنة · تحت 14 سنة".
- Chart values stay at opacity 0 until hover.
- مقارنة الفروع does not mark the branch selected in الفرع الحالي.
- The same wallet icon is used for أسعار الاشتراكات, الحسابات والمالية, and الميزانيات.
